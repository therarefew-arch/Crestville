# Changelog

The version shows at the bottom of the game screen. Each update adds 1.

## v0.0.13
- Day and night follow your phone's clock: dawn, day, dusk and night lighting, with a soft
  lantern glow around you at night.
- Weather changes every 20 minutes and suits each town: rain, storms, fog, wind, snow in
  Frostpeak and sandstorms in Sandglass Oasis.
- Wild creatures change with it: Shade creatures come out at night, Water in the rain, Spark in
  wind and storms, Stone in sandstorms. Rare visitors appear twice as often at night.
- The town label shows the time of day and weather.

## v0.0.12
- Breeding at the Hatchery: pick any two creatures as parents. The egg hatches right away as
  one of the parents' species. Stars mix the parents' (with a chance to beat both), each parent
  trait has a 45% chance to pass on (up to 3), the starting skill usually comes from the
  matching parent, and shiny parents make shiny babies far more likely.
- Parents aren't used up. Each breeding costs a little more.

## v0.0.11
- Expeditions: start one from any Travel Post (or Coach Driver Bea). Choose a region you've
  visited; each run is 6 stops and a boss, about 3 to 5 minutes.
- At every stop pick one of two paths: Wild pack, Wandering tamer, Treasure, Campfire (+attack
  and +HP for the rest of the run) or Shrine (choose one of three blessings: might, shelter,
  coins ×1.5 or XP ×1.5).
- Levels rise stop by stop; the boss pack is led by a strong alpha and always drops a charm
  (often Epic or Legendary).
- Losing ends the run but you keep everything. A summary shows coins, wins and charms.

## v0.0.10
- Town jobs: creatures resting at home (not on your team) can mine, fish or forage in towns
  you've visited. Later towns pay more, and matching elements work 50% faster. Helpers keep
  gathering while you're away, up to 8 hours, and slowly gain XP.
- Resources: ore, fish, herbs and rare gems (found while mining).
- Five skills that level to 99: Mining, Fishing, Foraging, Cooking and Smithing. Gathering
  skills raise yields and unlock more helper slots (3 up to 6).
- Workshop (Team → Craft): Sweet Berries, Level Stew (+1 level), Golden Feast (+3 levels),
  Forged Charms (better odds with higher Smithing) and Stardust.
- Team panel now has tabs: Team, Charms, Jobs and Craft.

## v0.0.09
- Charms: loot with random bonus stats, in Common, Rare, Epic and Legendary. 11 possible
  stats: attack, HP, % attack, % HP, a stronger first hit, bonus attack while first in line,
  thorns, healing after attacks, damage reduction, extra XP and extra coins. Better rarities
  roll more stats and bigger numbers; higher-level drops are stronger.
- Champions always drop a charm (often Epic or Legendary). Trainers drop one 40% of the time,
  Arena rivals 20%, wild creatures 5% (rare visitors 30%, shinies always).
- Loot reveal with spinning rays, a tumbling gem and stats popping in. Legendaries get a fanfare.
- Each creature wears up to 2 charms. New Charms tab in Team; equip from there or a creature's
  page. Salvage spare charms for stardust.

## v0.0.08
- Bicycles: buy one at any Market for 400 coins. Twice as fast as walking, with a bell.
- Creature mounts: ride any creature at Lv 20 or higher. Perks by type: water creatures and
  hovering creatures cross water, fire creatures cross lava, leaf and shade creatures halve wild
  battles, spark creatures are extra fast.
- New Ride button on the right Joy-Con to pick Walk, Bicycle or a creature.

## v0.0.07
- All 60 creatures redesigned to look more alien, while keeping the cozy style. Each species
  has its own fixed mix of: one big eye, two, three or four eyes, or eyes on stalks (all with
  colored irises and slit pupils); feet, four legs, tentacles, a slug foot, or hovering over a
  glowing ring; glowing spots, veins, rings or spirals; and mouths with tiny fangs, zigzag
  grins or none at all.
- Skin has a two-tone shimmer and contrasting accent colors.
- Animal ears became see-through fronds, swept-back fins or glowing orbs. Whiskers became
  feelers with glowing tips. Some species have glowing bumps along their backs.

## v0.0.06
- After every battle, XP bars fill with rolling numbers and rising ticks. A full bar bursts
  "LEVEL UP!", dings, bumps the level and keeps filling.
- Battle MVP: the creature that dealt the most damage gets a gold crown, with damage and K.O.s shown.
- Coins roll up in the top bar with a ding, a bounce and a floating "+N". Crests bounce when earned.
- Damage numbers scale with the size of the hit; big hits shake the arena with a thump, and
  finishing blows stamp "K.O.!".
- Level-up cards and evolutions roll attack, HP and level up from old to new values.

## v0.0.05
- Gentler walking speed. Pushing the stick all the way jogs a little faster.
- Level-ups get a celebration card: confetti, hopping creatures, the new level popping in,
  attack and HP gains, new skills, and a jingle.
- Evolution is a big event: the screen darkens with spinning light rays, the creature flickers
  between forms faster and faster with rising sound, then a white flash, a burst of stars, a
  fanfare and its new stats. Music dips during the moment.
- Evolved forms look more different: bigger, a determined look, and a glowing aura on final forms.
- Crest wins play a fanfare. New Sound effects toggle in Settings.

## v0.0.04
- Settings button at the top: music on/off, battle speed, save, and start a new game.
- Right Joy-Con is now a small Team button and a big A button.
- Town name shows as a label on the game screen.
- Town layouts are random for each new game: roads leave towns north, east, west or south, and
  buildings, ponds, grass, trees and people land in new spots. Every layout is checked so all
  roads, doors and grass connect. Your save keeps the same world every time you load it.
- Wild creatures get stronger the closer you get to a town's far gate, reaching about the
  Champion's level. Town signs show the level range.

## v0.0.03
- New controls: two Joy-Con halves. Drag the thumbstick to walk, push it all the way to run.
- Save button (also still saves automatically) and a Music on/off button.
- 10 new towns, 11 in total, linked in a chain north of Hollow Grove: Willowmere, Sunpetal Fields,
  Emberforge, Saltbreeze Harbor, Duskmire, Lanternhollow, Sandglass Oasis, Cogsworth, Frostpeak
  and Starfall Summit. Each has its own colors, trees, scenery, floating ambience, wild creatures,
  two trainers, a Champion and its own background music.
- 11 crests (one per Champion). The road to each next town opens once you hold the crest before it.
- Fast travel to visited towns from each town's Travel Post, or Coach Driver Bea in Crestville.
- Losing a battle now sends you to rest in the last town you visited.
- Battle music, and battle backgrounds match the town.

## v0.0.02
- Version number now shows in the game.
- Ties give XP. A tie with a trainer counts as a tie (not a loss) and doesn't send you home.

## v0.0.01 (earlier builds, before version numbers)
- Walk-around town, Meadow Route and Hollow Grove with tall-grass encounters, trainers and the Meadow Crest boss.
- Lineup battles: front fights front, next steps up. Scout and reorder before each fight.
- 60 creatures. Each rolls stars, 1-3 traits, a starting skill and a rare shiny color.
- Levels up to 100, XP from knockouts, up to 4 skills learned as they level.
- Merging, stardust rerolls, Meadow and Mystery eggs, Market, Arena with rivals and friend codes.
