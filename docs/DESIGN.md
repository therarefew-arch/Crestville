# Crestville — Game Concept (draft)

**Pitch:** Collect creatures, build a team of 5, and watch them battle on their own.
Your team keeps fighting and earning while you're away. Come back, collect rewards,
tweak the team, push further.

Working title: Crestville. Platform: mobile web (HTML5), installable to the home screen.

## Core loop
1. **Battle** – Your team fights waves automatically. No tapping needed.
2. **Earn** – Wins give coins, XP and creature eggs. Earnings continue offline (capped at ~8 hours).
3. **Build** – Hatch eggs, level up creatures, swap team members, equip charms.
4. **Push** – Beat a zone boss to unlock the next region of Crestville.

## The world
Crestville is a hill town surrounded by wild regions. Each region has its own creatures
and a "Crest" (badge) guarded by a boss tamer. Collecting all crests is the long-term goal.
- Meadow Hollow → Ember Ridge → Tidepool Coast → Whisperwood → Stormcrest Peak

## Creatures
- Original designs (no existing franchise characters).
- **6 elements** in a loop: Leaf > Water > Fire > Leaf, plus Spark, Stone, Shade.
- **Roles:** Tank, Striker, Support, Trickster.
- **Evolution:** 3 stages, unlocked by level + an item from the matching region.
- **Rarity:** Common, Rare, Epic, Legendary.

## Team building (the main decision)
- 5 slots: 2 front row, 3 back row. Front row takes hits first.
- **Synergies:** 2/3/4 creatures of one element or role give team bonuses
  (e.g. 3 Fire = +20% burn damage; 2 Support = heal every 5s).
- Each creature has 1 passive and 1 auto-cast skill that fires when its energy fills.

## Idle & progression
- Offline earnings screen on return ("While you were away…").
- **Town upgrades:** Crestville itself grows — Hatchery (faster eggs), Training Yard
  (passive XP), Market (more coins), Lab (unlock synergies).
- Daily quests and a login streak.
- Optional "speed up 2x" toggle while the app is open.

## First playable version (MVP)
- 1 region, ~12 creatures, 3 elements.
- Auto-battle screen with simple animations.
- Team editor (drag into 5 slots), egg hatching, leveling.
- Offline earnings, save in the browser.
- Portrait layout, one-thumb controls.
