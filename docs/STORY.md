# Halcyon Noir — Story Bible

An original noir mecha story. Inspired by the mood of late-90s Toonami anime: rain, jazz,
art deco towers, a city with no past, and giant machines. No names, designs or lines from
any existing show.

## Tone rules
- Restrained. Characters say less than they mean. No speeches, no winks at the player.
- Specific images over big words ("four grooves, deep as my arm" not "terrifying claw marks").
- Humor is dry and rare. Nobody quips during danger.
- The mystery is answered in small pieces, and every answer opens a bigger question.

## The city: Halcyon
- Forty years ago, the whole city woke up on the same morning with no memory of anything
  before it. Names, faces, history: gone. People call it **the Blank**.
- What remained: the streets, the rain, art deco towers nobody remembers building, and
  enormous rusted machines in the harbor that nobody can explain.
- **The Halcyon Trust** runs the city from the Tower. People who talk too much about
  "Before" tend to end up talking to the Trust instead.
- Most people have learned not to dig.

## Elias Vane (the player)
- A "recoverer": finds lost things for a fee. Rings, ledgers, people.
- Calm, observant, tired. Wears a dark suit and hat. Doesn't carry a gun.
- Narrates in first person, past tense.
- Under the old foundry sleeps a towering machine that answers only to him: **the Graven**.
  He has never asked why. The game will.

## The Graven
- A giant art deco war machine: heavy shoulders, a visor slit that glows amber, piston
  forearms that hiss steam. Slow, enormous, every punch a ton of steel.
- Runs hot. Push it too hard and it locks up in a cloud of steam.

## Relics
- Machines from before the Blank, waking up one by one. Each seems to be *guarding*
  something.

## Episode 1 — Low Tide (the playable slice)
**Hook:** Mara Quill, a lounge singer at the Blue Lantern, hires Vane to find her brother
Teodor, missing three nights. Before he vanished, Teo claimed he *remembered something from
before the Blank*.

**Clues on the block**
1. **The photograph** (from Mara): a lighthouse on a rocky point. Halcyon has no
   lighthouse. On the back: "It's still lit."
2. **The pawn ticket** (from Ostrowski, the bartender): Teo pawned an old brass key
   stamped with a wave, then bought it back the night he vanished.
3. **Gouges on the pier**: four grooves, scorched, running down into the water.
4. **The tide gate**: rusted shut since the Blank, but the lock has been scraped clean.
   The keyhole is stamped with a wave.
5. **Gil's confession** (the harbor watchman): at first he lies that Teo left on a fishing
   boat. Confront him with the gouges and he admits Teo opened the tide gate, the water lit
   up green-gold, and Teo walked into it.

**The deduction** (one chance, no retries)
- Where did Teodor go? → He opened the tide gate with the key and walked into the water.
- What was the light? → Something from before the Blank, and it's awake.
- Both right (**Sharp**): Vane knows where to hit it. The Relic's core is marked and
  exposed-core hits do triple damage.
- One wrong (**Shaken**): the Graven starts with 10% less armor.
- Both wrong (**Lost**): 20% less armor, and you go in blind.

**The Relic: the Tidewarden.** A vast crab-like machine with a lantern for a heart. It
rises from the harbor as Vane finishes his deduction.

**Ending:** inside the Tidewarden's core is a brass lighthouse lens. It plays Teo's
voice: "Mara, it's still lit. There's another city under the water. I'm going to see it."
Teo is alive, somewhere below. And the things that were forgotten are starting to
remember.

**The Graven thread (the real mystery):** the Tidewarden hesitates when it sees the Graven, as
if it recognizes it. The lens carries a second recording: "Warden to Graven. The gate is open.
Come home." Nobody but Vane has ever said that name. Inside the cockpit, the same wave mark from
the tide gate is stamped above the controls, worn smooth by someone else's hand. The question
the whole series turns on: who sat in the Graven before Vane, and where is home?
